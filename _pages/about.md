Hey, thanks for stopping by! 👋

I am a final-year Civil Engineering student at the [University of Engineering and Technology (UTEC)](https://utec.edu.pe/), Peru, and currently work as a Project Control Intern at [COSAPI S.A.](https://www.cosapi.com.pe/site/index.aspx), one of the leading construction companies in the country. I am a recipient of the Beca 18 scholarship (2021/26), awarded by the Peruvian government for academic excellence, and I participated in the GCI Global Program in Data Science and AI at the [Matsuo-Iwasawa Lab](https://weblab.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/) at the University of Tokyo.

My research focuses on robust artificial intelligence and computer vision systems for complex and dynamic construction environments, including domain shift mitigation, explainability in deep learning models, UAV-based monitoring, and intelligent construction state representation under real-world operational conditions.

Currently, I am conducting research on UAV-based deep learning systems for automated monitoring of construction phases in anchored shotcrete retaining walls under real-world construction site conditions. My research paper, *“Deep Learning-Based Intelligent Monitoring Framework for Anchored Shotcrete Retaining Walls: A Case Study of a Large-Scale Clinic Project in Lima, Peru”*, was accepted at the International Conference on Computing in Civil Engineering [(i3CE 2026)](https://www.i3ce2026.co), organized by the [Seoul National University Smart Construction AI Research Center](https://scai.snu.ac.kr/) and the American Society of Civil Engineers [(ASCE)](https://ascelibrary.org/). As part of the conference, I was invited to present the work through both an oral presentation and an academic poster session in Songdo, Incheon, South Korea.

My work integrates Construction AI, machine learning, BIM/VDC, and data-driven approaches for productivity, planning, and project control within Project Production Management (PPM), Lean Construction, and Modern Construction frameworks. I am also interested in multimodal representation of construction processes, intelligent monitoring, and AI systems applied to construction operations.

Beyond research, I have participated in technical and student leadership initiatives related to construction engineering and digital construction technologies, including serving as Founder and President of the [ACI UTEC Student Chapter](https://www.concrete.org/) (2023/25).

Outside engineering, I enjoy going to the gym, practicing running, and continuously improving my English proficiency. If you are interested in collaboration, research, or AI-driven construction technology initiatives, feel free to reach out.
