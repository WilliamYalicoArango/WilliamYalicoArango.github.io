Hey, thanks for stopping by! 👋

I am a final-year Civil Engineering student at the [University of Engineering and Technology (UTEC)](https://utec.edu.pe/), Peru, and currently work as a Project Control Intern at [COSAPI S.A.](https://www.cosapi.com.pe/site/index.aspx), one of the leading construction companies in the country. I am also a recipient of the Beca 18 scholarship (2021/26), awarded by the Peruvian government for academic excellence.

I participated in the GCI Global Program in Data Science and AI at the [Matsuo-Iwasawa Lab](https://weblab.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/) at the University of Tokyo, and I am currently enrolled in the AWS AI & ML Scholars Program.

My interests focus on Construction AI, computer vision, machine learning, BIM/VDC, and data-driven approaches for productivity, planning, and project control within Project Production Management (PPM), Lean Construction, and Modern Construction frameworks.

Currently, I am conducting research on UAV-based deep learning systems for automated monitoring of construction phases in anchored shotcrete retaining walls under real construction site conditions. My research paper, *“Deep Learning-Based Intelligent Monitoring Framework for Anchored Shotcrete Retaining Walls: A Case Study of a Large-Scale Clinic Project in Lima, Peru”*, was accepted at the International Conference on Computing in Civil Engineering [(i3CE 2026)](https://www.i3ce2026.co), organized by the [Seoul National University Smart Construction AI Research Center](https://scai.snu.ac.kr/) and the American Society of Civil Engineers [(ASCE)](https://ascelibrary.org/).

As part of the conference, I was invited to present the work through both an oral presentation and an academic poster session in Songdo, Incheon, South Korea.

Beyond research, I have participated in technical and student leadership initiatives related to construction engineering and digital construction technologies, including serving as Founder and President of the [ACI UTEC Student Chapter](https://www.concrete.org/) (2023/25).

Outside engineering, I enjoy going to the gym, practicing running, and continuously improving my English proficiency. If you are interested in collaboration, research, or AI-driven construction technology initiatives, feel free to reach out.
