Hey, thanks for stopping by! 👋

I am a final-year Civil Engineering student at the University of Engineering and Technology [(UTEC)](https://utec.edu.pe/), Peru, and currently work as a Project Control Intern at [COSAPI S.A.](https://www.cosapi.com.pe/site/index.aspx), one of the leading construction companies in Peru. I am a recipient of the Beca 18 scholarship (2021/26), awarded by the Peruvian government for academic excellence, and I have participated in the GCI Global Program in Data Science and AI at the [Matsuo-Iwasawa Lab](https://weblab.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/) at the University of Tokyo. I am also currently enrolled in the AWS AI & ML Scholars Program and was selected as a scholar for the [Aspire Leaders Program](https://www.aspireleaders.org/about/) (Cohort 1, 2025).

My work and interests are focused on Construction AI, machine learning, computer vision, BIM/VDC, Lean Construction, Pull Planning, and data-driven approaches for productivity, planning, and project control within Project Production Management (PPM) and Modern Construction frameworks.

Currently, I am researching UAV-based deep learning systems for automated monitoring of construction phases in anchored shotcrete retaining walls under real construction site conditions. My paper, *“Deep Learning-Based Intelligent Monitoring Framework for Anchored Shotcrete Retaining Walls: A Case Study of a Large-Scale Clinic Project in Lima, Peru”*, was accepted at the International Conference on Computing in Civil Engineering [(i3CE 2026)](https://www.i3ce2026.co), organized by the [Seoul National University Smart Construction AI Research Center](https://scai.snu.ac.kr/) and the American Society of Civil Engineers [(ASCE)](https://ascelibrary.org/). As part of the conference, I was invited to present the work through both an oral presentation and an academic poster session in Songdo, Incheon, South Korea.

Beyond research, I have participated in technical and student leadership initiatives related to construction engineering and digital construction technologies, including serving as Founder and President of the [ACI UTEC Student Chapter](https://www.concrete.org/) (2023/25).

Outside engineering, I enjoy going to the gym, practicing running, and continuously improving my English proficiency. If you are interested in collaboration, research, or construction technology initiatives, feel free to reach out.
